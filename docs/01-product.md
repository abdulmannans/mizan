# 01 — Product

## Problem

An Indian Muslim (or ethical) investor already knows the short list of Direct Growth “Ethical / Shariah” mutual funds. The hard parts are:

1. Keep a **monthly SIP** going.
2. Know when a **real drawdown** might justify extra lumpsum — without treating every quiet day as a buy.
3. Hold **gold / silver** as the other sleeve.
4. See, hypothetically, what skipped dip-days would be worth **today**.
5. Get that signal **on the phone**, even if they never open a website.

## Solution

**Mizan** is a **standalone Android app**:

- Bundled catalog of **Direct Plan Growth** ethical/Shariah-*named* open-ended funds.
- Watchlist + optional SIP amount and debit day (stored on device).
- **On-device** daily pipeline: fetch NAV/prices → score 0–100 → notify if 65+.
- **Missed opportunities** from local score history (after first-run backfill).
- Gold ₹/10g and silver ₹/kg, plus gold **₹10,000 off the ~60-day high**.

## What Mizan is not

- Not a module of any other site or admin panel.
- Not a broker. No KYC, no UPI, no order button.
- Not a stock picker. Do not rank the five ethical funds as “best today”.
- Not a certified Shariah board. Labels are a curated personal screen.
- Not dependent on email (Brevo) or a gym user table.
