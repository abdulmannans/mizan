# 02 — Principles

These are product rules, not slogans. Do not invert them to “get more alerts”.

## P1 — SIP is the default engine

A Systematic Investment Plan on a screened scheme is the core strategy (method is neutral; permissibility depends on the **scheme**). The app reminds, estimates allotment NAV, and never tells the user to pause SIPs because the dip score is low.

## P2 — The 0–100 number is a dip meter, not a ranking

The score answers: **how beaten-up is this NAV vs its own recent history and Nifty?**

It does **not** answer: which ethical fund should I own.

Tata Ethical and Taurus Ethical often print similar scores on the same crash. That is expected. They are one **equity sleeve**.

Copy in the app: **“Dip opportunity”**, never “Rank #1” or “Best fund”.

## P3 — Extra cash only at 65+

| Score | Level | Extra lumpsum |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | Unattractive | 0% |
| 30–49 | Weak | 0% |
| 50–64 | Neutral | 0–10% (mid 5%) — **no notification, not a missed deploy** |
| 65–79 | Attractive | 20–30% (mid 25%) — **alerts + missed clusters** |
| 80–89 | Very attractive | 30–50% (mid 40%) |
| 90–100 | Exceptional | 50–75% (mid 63%) |

Do **not** add 45+ or 50–64 to push / missed-dip lists. Neutral is ordinary market noise. Successful long-running Islamic portfolios (Amana/Saturna: investors not speculators; Wahed: automated contributions by risk profile) do not ping every soft day.

## P4 — Cluster crashes

Attractive days on **one** fund within **7 days** of each other are **one** event. Keep the **highest score** day as the would-have-deployed print. Same idea for notifications (cooldown; only notify again if the **level** steps up, e.g. attractive → very attractive).

## P5 — Hypothetical only

Suggested ₹ = allocation mid-% × **available lumpsum pool** (default ₹50,000).  
What-if ₹10,000 is a **fixed** reference on each missed cluster.  
No trades are recorded. Always show the disclaimer.

## P6 — Gold is the diversifier

In India, retail sukuk is thin. Gold/silver is the other sleeve. Metal drop alerts are **independent** of the 0–100 score (gold: ₹10,000 off the ~60-day high).

## P7 — Time in market beats calling bottoms

Vanguard-style evidence: for money already in hand, lump-sum usually beats waiting in cash. Dip top-ups are an **overlay** for ring-fenced extra cash, not a reason to sit out SIPs. If the user cannot fund a crash, the overlay is worthless — say that in empty states.
