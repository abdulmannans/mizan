# 09 — Compliance and copy

## Canonical disclaimer (must ship verbatim unless legal revises it)

> Shariah / halal labels in this app are curated screens for personal use. They are not a fatwa, not a certified Shariah-board ruling, and not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.

Show on: first launch (acknowledge), Home, Fund detail, Missed, Metals, notification “more” / long text.

## Required phrases

- Analytical signal, **not investment advice**
- Hypothetical / would-have — never “you earned”
- Continue SIPs independently
- The fund can fall further
- Gold/silver: Mumbai-style quotes; **excludes making charges and GST**
- AMFI “Ethical/Shariah” in the **name** is not a Shariah-board certificate

## Play Store

- Category: Finance
- Not a trading app; no real-money execution
- Privacy: data stays on device (watchlist, NAV history, scores). No account server in v1. Market HTTP only (MFAPI, Yahoo, metal/FX).
- Uninstall deletes local data. Optional export in v2.

## SEBI / advice

This is a **personal analytics** tool. Do not use “recommend”, “assured”, “multibagger”, or implied portfolio management for others. Suggested ₹ is a **sizing band**, not a directive.

## Shariah honesty

Do not badge “Halal certified” unless `shariah_verification_status = verified_compliant` **and** copy still says personal screen / not a fatwa. Unreviewed funds: gray “Unreviewed”.
